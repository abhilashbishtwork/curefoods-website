Company FAQ

What is Curefoods?
Curefoods is a Bengaluru-headquartered house of food brands, founded in 2020 by Ankit Nagori. It builds and scales multiple F&B brands — spanning healthy meals, biryani, pizza, desserts and celebration cakes — out of a shared network of cloud kitchens and dine-in stores across India.
How many brands does Curefoods own?
Curefoods' published brand portfolio includes Kitchens of Eatfit, Sharief Bhai, Olio Pizza, Arambam, Nomad Pizza, CakeZone and Frozen Bottle, plus master-franchise partner Krispy Kreme.
What is a 'house of brands' model, and why does Curefoods use it?
A house-of-brands model runs several distinct, independently-branded products from shared infrastructure — in Curefoods' case, shared cloud kitchens, supply chain and delivery operations. It lets one kitchen serve several occasions (health food, biryani, dessert) to the same customer without diluting any single brand's identity.
How many kitchens and stores does Curefoods operate?
As of March 31, 2025, Curefoods runs 281 cloud kitchens, 99 kiosks and 122 restaurants across India.
Who founded Curefoods and when?
Curefoods was founded in 2020 by Ankit Nagori, former co-founder of Cure.fit (Cult.fit) and former Chief Business Officer of Flipkart, who set out to apply the same operating rigor to the food-brands business.
How can I order from Curefoods brands?
Every Curefoods brand is listed on major food delivery platforms (Swiggy, Zomato) under its own name — search the brand name directly, e.g. 'Eatfit' or 'Sharief Bhai' — and select brands also operate dedicated apps and dine-in stores.
Is Curefoods the same company as Rebel Foods?
No. Curefoods and Rebel Foods are separate, competing Indian cloud-kitchen companies that both use a house-of-brands model. Rebel Foods owns brands like Faasos, Behrouz Biryani and Oven Story; Curefoods owns Eatfit, Sharief Bhai, CakeZone and the brands listed on this site.
Does Curefoods have physical, dine-in stores or only delivery kitchens?
Both. Most Curefoods brands operate delivery-only cloud kitchens, but several — including Sharief Bhai's Mosque Road flagship and Krispy Kreme's dine-in cafés — also run dine-in and walk-in retail formats.
How do I apply for a job at Curefoods?
Open roles across all Curefoods brands are listed on the Careers page (curefoods.in/careers.html); applications can also be sent to careers@curefoods.in.
How can franchise or partnership enquiries reach Curefoods?
Franchise and partnership enquiries can be sent to hello@curefoods.in.
Are Curefoods' kitchens FSSAI-licensed and hygienic?
Yes. Every food business operating in India, including cloud kitchens listed on Swiggy or Zomato, is required to hold a valid FSSAI licence, and Curefoods' kitchens operate under that same regulatory requirement.
What should I do if my order from a Curefoods brand has a problem?
For orders placed through Swiggy or Zomato, use that platform's order-help or refund flow first, since they process the payment and delivery. For orders placed directly with a brand (its own app, website or phone line), contact that brand's listed support channel, or reach Curefoods at support@curefoods.in.
Do Curefoods brands offer vegetarian and eggless options?
Yes, across the portfolio — CakeZone runs a 100% eggless cake menu, Krispy Kreme India uses an eggless doughnut recipe, and Eatfit, Sharief Bhai and Arambam all carry dedicated vegetarian dishes alongside non-vegetarian ones.
Can I track my order from a Curefoods brand in real time?
Orders placed via Swiggy or Zomato are tracked inside those apps. Brands with their own ordering channels, such as Sharief Bhai and Krispy Kreme India, offer tracking through their own app or website as well.

Brand FAQ

Eatfit

What makes Eatfit different from a regular food delivery brand?
Every Eatfit dish is nutrition-labelled with exact calories, protein, carbs and fat, designed with dietitians so customers can hit fitness goals without home-cooking.
What is 'Kitchens of Eatfit'?
Kitchens of Eatfit is the shared-kitchen umbrella that houses Eatfit alongside seven sister brands — HRX by Eatfit, Great Indian Khichdi, Homeplate, Chaat Street, Rolls on Wheels, Millet Express and Madras Curd Rice Company — so one order can mix dishes from all of them.
Which cities is Eatfit available in?
Eatfit operates in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai and Delhi NCR, with kitchens expanding as Curefoods opens new cloud-kitchen clusters.
Is Eatfit good for weight loss?
Eatfit portions and labels every meal with exact calories, protein, carbs and fat, which makes it easier to track intake toward a weight or fitness goal. As with any meal plan, results depend on overall diet and activity, not any single meal.
Does Eatfit have both vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals?
Yes — Eatfit's menu spans both, from grilled chicken and Peri Peri chicken rice bowls to paneer and quinoa khichdi options, all nutrition-labelled the same way.
Who is Eatfit's brand ambassador?
Actor Hrithik Roshan is Eatfit's brand ambassador and an investor in the brand.
Can I order Eatfit as a one-time meal or only as a subscription?
Eatfit meals can be ordered à la carte through Swiggy, Zomato or Eatfit's own app and website — there's no subscription requirement, though many customers use it for regular, everyday meal planning.

Sharief Bhai

Where is Sharief Bhai's flagship store?
Sharief Bhai's flagship dine-in store is on Mosque Road in Frazer Town, Bengaluru — the city's historic biryani and old-city food street.
Is Sharief Bhai available outside Karnataka?
Sharief Bhai runs 54 stores across 10 cities in Karnataka (Bengaluru, Mysore, Mangalore, Manipal, Tumkur, Hassan), Tamil Nadu (Chennai, Coimbatore, Hosur) and Puducherry — it does not currently have stores in Hyderabad or the rest of Telangana.
What is the difference between Sharief Bhai and Roz Shawarma?
Roz Shawarma is Sharief Bhai's sibling brand focused only on shawarma and rolls, sharing kitchens but running a lighter, faster menu for quick-bite occasions.
Is Sharief Bhai's meat halal?
Yes — Sharief Bhai uses 100% halal meat across all its outlets.
Does Sharief Bhai have vegetarian options?
Yes — alongside its biryani and mandi range, Sharief Bhai serves vegetarian dishes such as ghee rice, soya chaap biryani and paneer-based items.
Is Sharief Bhai available outside India?
Sharief Bhai opened its first international outlet at BurJuman Mall in Dubai in November 2024, marking the brand's expansion into the UAE alongside its home market in India.

Olio

What is Enso and how is it related to Olio?
Enso is a related, smaller-format concept run out of select Olio kitchens as Curefoods tests new menu formats; it is not available in every city Olio serves.
Which cities is Olio available in?
Olio operates in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune, Kolkata and Ahmedabad, across roughly 200 kitchens.
Is Olio pizza made fresh to order?
Yes — Olio's positioning is built around cooking pizza and pasta fresh per order rather than pre-batching or reheating.

Arambam

What does '5 states in 1 plate' mean for Arambam?
It's Arambam's positioning line — a single menu spanning South Indian regional styles across five states, built around millets instead of rice or wheat.
Is Arambam the same company as Millet Express?
Arambam is run by Curefoods' Millet Express subsidiary, which also operates the separate Millet Express brand; the two share ownership but run distinct menus and storefronts.
Why does Arambam use millets instead of rice or wheat?
Millets generally have a lower glycaemic index and higher fibre content than white rice or refined wheat, which is the nutritional case Arambam builds its menu around. This is general dietary information, not medical advice.
Which cities is Arambam available in?
Arambam operates in Bengaluru, Mumbai and Hyderabad, having launched its first restaurant in Hyderabad in April 2024.

Krispy Kreme

Is Krispy Kreme a Curefoods-owned brand or a franchise?
Krispy Kreme is a global brand; Curefoods operates it in India as master franchisee, running local stores and delivery under Krispy Kreme's international recipes and standards rather than owning the underlying IP.
When did Krispy Kreme first come to India?
Krispy Kreme first opened in India in Bengaluru in January 2013 under an earlier operator. Curefoods later became the brand's exclusive franchisee across India.
Are Krispy Kreme doughnuts in India vegetarian or eggless?
Krispy Kreme adapted its recipe for India with an eggless doughnut formulation, reflecting the vegetarian and egg-free dietary preferences common among Indian customers.
Which cities is Krispy Kreme available in?
Krispy Kreme runs 150+ stores across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Delhi NCR, Jaipur and Chandigarh under Curefoods.

Nomad Pizza

What makes Nomad Pizza different from other pizza delivery brands?
Nomad Pizza hand-stretches every base to order and builds its menu around globally-inspired toppings like Peri Peri and Korean BBQ alongside classics.
Who founded Nomad Pizza?
Nomad Pizza was founded by Amit Ajwani in Delhi in 2019, before Curefoods acquired the brand in October 2021.
Which cities is Nomad Pizza available in?
Nomad Pizza operates in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai and Delhi NCR.

CakeZone

Does CakeZone deliver at midnight?
Yes — CakeZone offers midnight and same-day delivery slots designed for last-minute birthday and anniversary celebrations.
Does CakeZone offer eggless cakes?
Yes — CakeZone carries a full menu of 100% eggless cakes across its flavours, not a limited add-on selection.
How fast can CakeZone deliver a cake?
Beyond same-day and midnight slots, CakeZone offers express delivery within 60 minutes in select areas.
Can I personalize a CakeZone cake with a name or message?
Yes — CakeZone lets customers add a name or message at checkout on select cakes for personalization.
Who is CakeZone's brand ambassador?
Actor Nora Fatehi is CakeZone's brand ambassador and an investor in the brand.

Frozen Bottle

Is Frozen Bottle vegetarian?
Yes — Frozen Bottle's entire menu, from milkshakes to ice-cream sundae jars, is 100% vegetarian.
Are Frozen Bottle's desserts eggless?
Yes — all Frozen Bottle products are 100% eggless.
What does Frozen Bottle sell besides milkshakes?
Beyond its signature thick milkshakes, Frozen Bottle serves ice-cream sundae jars, boba and bubble teas, cold coffee and Belgian waffles.
Which cities is Frozen Bottle available in?
Frozen Bottle has outlets across Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Delhi NCR and Kolkata, with Bengaluru as its largest market.
